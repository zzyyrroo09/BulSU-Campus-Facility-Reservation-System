### 1. Statement of Principle & Institutional Compliance
In compliance with the **BulSU AI Policy**, institutional research ethics guidelines, and internationally recognized academic standards (IEEE / ACM / Elsevier AI Transparency Guidelines), this document explicitly discloses the extent, nature, and purpose of generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools utilized during the conception, development, and documentation of the **Campus Facility Reservation and Event Scheduling System**.

---

### 2. Tools & Technologies Employed
- **AI Tool Utilized:** Google DeepMind / Antigravity (Powered by Gemini Models)
- **Role of Tool:** Pair-programming assistant, architectural sounding board, and documentation formatting tool.
- **Human Oversight:** 100% human-verified. All architectural choices, business rules, database schemas, and source code were directed, tested, audited, and defended by the student group members.

---

### 3. Detailed Disclosure of AI-Assisted Components

| Project Component | Nature of AI Assistance | Human Verification & Contribution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Architectural Scaffolding** | Assisted in generating initial Django boilerplate templates conforming to the strict prohibition of `django.contrib.admin`. | Reviewed directory structures, URL routing, and ensured monolithic MTV structure conformed to syllabus requirements. |
| **Mathematical Interval Logic** | Assisted in drafting helper functions for half-open interval $[start, end)$ collision logic in `services.py`. | Formulated the interval math ($start_A < end_B \land end_A > start_B$), tested adjacent edge cases, and validated database query logic. |
| **Concurrency Locking Implementation** | Suggested syntax patterns for Django's `select_for_update()` within `transaction.atomic()` blocks. | Verified row-level locking behavior on target records, added SQLite concurrency timeouts, and proved race condition prevention. |
| **Automated Testing Suite** | Drafted initial assertions for unit and integration tests in `reservations/tests.py`. | Executed test runs, verified failure conditions for competing approvals, and validated test database tear-downs. |
| **Seed Data Generation** | Generated realistic fictional data templates (fictional student names, campus facilities, and amenities). | Verified data complied with the requirement to use purely fictional identities with no real student personal data exposed. |
| **Documentation & Diagramming** | Assisted in structuring Markdown files and generating Mermaid.js diagram syntax for the MTV flowchart, ERD, and State Machine. | Authored domain explanations, validated ERD entity cardinalities, and tailored presentation cues for each group member. |

---

### 4. Components Hand-Crafted & Audited by the Group
The following core engineering decisions were strictly determined and verified by the student development team:
1. **Zero-Admin Architecture**: The decision to manually implement custom admin views, forms, navigation badges, and tables under `/manage/`.
2. **Data Privacy Safeguard**: Designing the user availability view to scrub private organizer identities and event memos, complying with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).
3. **Session Invalidation Logic**: Creating custom middleware (`AccountActiveCheckMiddleware`) to terminate existing sessions when an account is marked inactive.
4. **Physical Operational Slips**: Designing and formatting the official printable BulSU Facility Reservation Slip and Completion Summary.

We, the student authors of this project, affirm that:
- AI tools were utilized strictly as cognitive and productivity aids, not as substitutes for understanding and mastering web programming principles.
- No confidential university data, actual student records, or unauthorized copyrighted materials were inputted into or generated by AI tools.
- Every group member understands the submitted codebase and takes full personal and collective responsibility for defending every line of code during the Week 17 Demonstration and Q&A with Dr. Dela Rosa.
